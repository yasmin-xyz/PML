# PickMeLabs

PickMeLabs is an AI visibility research lab for ecommerce. As more shoppers ask AI tools directly for product recommendations instead of searching Google, brands have little visibility into — or control over — whether and how they get recommended. PickMeLabs researches how AI models surface, compare, and rank products, and helps ecommerce brands understand and improve their visibility in AI-generated answers.

**Live site:** [pickmelabs.com](https://pickmelabs.com)

## What This Is

A mix of applied research and a working product site:

- **Research** — case studies and experiments on how AI models recommend products in specific niches (e.g. protein powder, mattresses), looking for patterns in what gets surfaced and why
- **Concepts** — a framework for understanding AI recommendation behavior, built around three recurring patterns: consensus anchors, constraint filtering, and specialization narrowing
- **AI Visibility Experiment** — a 2–3 week engagement where a brand's current AI visibility gets audited and tested against these patterns

## Tech Stack

- Plain HTML / CSS / JavaScript (no framework — kept intentionally simple and fast)
- Hosted on Hostinger
- JSON-LD structured data for AI/search discoverability

## Site Structure

- `index.html` — homepage
- `research.html` — case studies and findings
- `concepts.html` — the three-pattern framework, with custom SVG diagrams
- `about.html`
- AI Basics hub + article pages covering how AI recommendation systems work (not live yet)

## Why This Exists

Traditional SEO answers "how do I rank on Google." There's little research on "how do I get recommended by ChatGPT, Claude, or Perplexity when someone asks for a product recommendation." PickMeLabs is my attempt to build real, tested answers to that question rather than repackaging old SEO advice with "AI" in the title.
